Warnings before you open a dangerous site

When warnings are on, Artemis checks each site you open and shows a warning page instead of sites that are reported as phishing or look like they're impersonating a known brand.

What this sends: the domain of each site you open (for example shop.example.com) goes to the Artemis server at . The full address, page content, and anything you type are never sent.

What stays in your browser: results are kept for 6 hours so the same site isn't checked twice, and are cleared when you close the browser.

Read the privacy policy. You can turn warnings off any time from the Artemis button in your toolbar.
