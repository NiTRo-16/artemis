What Artemis does

Artemis runs passive checks on a domain or link you enter. It reads what an ordinary visitor's browser would receive: the site's certificate, response headers and cookies, plus public DNS records, registration details and public blocklists. It also opens the page in an isolated browser to see forms that the page's scripts create. It doesn't type into forms, log in to sites, look for hidden pages or test for vulnerabilities. The Artemis Site Check browser extension, if you turn its warnings on, runs a lighter check on the domain of each site you open.
